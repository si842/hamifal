## The Thesis

**OUR THESIS**

## The economics of building companies changed.

Software and AI make it possible for small, experienced teams to build and operate far more than before.

HAMIFAL is built around a few simple principles.

**01 Start with the customer**
Get close to customers early.
Build with real problems, real users, and real feedback from the beginning.
Product and go-to-market should develop together.

**02 Focus is the advantage**
Building more is easy. Choosing what matters is hard.
Work on fewer problems. Understand them deeply. Ignore the rest.

**03 Domain expertise matters**
Knowing what to build matters more as building gets easier.
Work with people who understand the problem, the customer, and the industry from the inside.

**04 Stay small**
Keep teams small for as long as possible.
Add people when they increase output, not because the company is growing.

**05 Use technology everywhere**
Technology should improve the whole company, not only the product.
Use software and AI wherever they reduce work, improve decisions, or increase output.

**06 Earn complexity**
Do not add structure before it is needed.
Process, management, roles, and systems should solve real constraints.
Complexity should follow the business.
